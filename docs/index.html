Features

A music player, and nothing else.

  1. 01

    A Home made for you

    Rows for your country and language, a Made for you row, and picks for the time of day. They come from the songs you play through, replay and skip, and are worked out on your phone.

  2. 02

    Recommendations you control

    The Daily Mix and the endless queue follow what you really listen to and learn from what you skip. Choose Don't play this on any song or artist, and Hush leaves it out. You can clear what it learned in Settings.

  3. 03

    Daily Mix and listening stats

    A fresh mix every day, plus your top songs and artists. Plays are counted on your phone and never uploaded.

  4. 04

    Your library stays yours

    Liked songs and playlists live on your device. Back up everything, including your taste and settings, to a file locked with a password (AES-256), or import a public YouTube or Spotify playlist from its link.

  5. 05

    Made to look good

    A dark theme and a light theme, seven accent colours, and a player that takes its colours from the cover art.

  6. 06

    Easy to reach

    Lock-screen controls, home-screen widgets, an editable queue, and a sleep timer from 15 to 60 minutes or the end of the song.

  7. 07

    DJ mode

    A DJ tab with an endless list of club-friendly songs picked from what you listen to. Search and queue your own songs, and Hush mixes them on the beat with bass swaps, rolls and echo-outs. DJ mode keeps its own history and stats, separate from normal listening. Songs are prepared on your phone and deleted when the set ends.

Left out

No ads. No accounts. No analytics. Nothing to sign up for, nothing collecting data.

Privacy

Built to collect nothing.

No account. There is no sign-in and no Google login.

No analytics, ads or crash reporting. Hush has no servers of its own.

Your data stays on your phone. Likes, playlists, stats and what Hush learned about your taste are stored on the device, and uninstalling Hush deletes them.

Two outside requests. Hush asks YouTube for songs, and every few hours asks GitHub whether a new version is out. You can switch the update check off in Settings.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. 01

    Download

    Open the latest release on GitHub and download the .apk file under Assets.

  2. 02

    Install

    Open the file on your phone. If Android asks, allow installing from unknown sources.

  3. 03

    Allow notifications

    Open Hush and allow notifications, so playback controls can show on your lock screen.

New versions install over the old one and keep your liked songs and playlists.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Hush free?

Yes. It is open source under the GPLv3, and the full code is on GitHub.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. Hush is for Android 8.0 and newer.

Do I need a YouTube or Google account?

No. Hush has no sign-in at all. You can play, search and make playlists without one.

Is Hush made by YouTube?

No. Hush is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC or YouTube.
